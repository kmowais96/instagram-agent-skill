# Hook bank: @thetazkiyyahguide carousels

The cover title is the hook. It has to do one of three jobs in under two seconds:
**name a mistake the reader is making**, **promise a fix they want**, or **open a
question they need closed**. Every hook below does one of those.

## Rules for a hook on this page

1. 5 to 10 words. Fits in two lines at cover size.
2. Speaks to the reader's life (phone, Fajr, school, anger, friends, faith dips), not a topic heading. "Sabr" is a heading. "Mistakes we make when we're tested" is a hook.
3. One concrete noun the reader can picture: the phone, the 3am scroll, the friend, the first hour.
4. Honest. No "This will change your life", no "99% of Muslims", no number you can't back up.
5. Never shames. "Mistakes *we* make", not "Why *you* are failing".
6. Has a source ready. If the slides behind the hook can't be backed by a sahih or hasan hadith or an ayah, the hook gets rewritten, not the hadith stretched.
7. Change the wording for this page. Don't copy another creator's lines.

**Score each hook before using it** (keep it only at 4/5 or 5/5):
specific to youth · one picture in the head · a reason to swipe · sounds like a person talking · the slides can prove it.
