# Reel rules

Standing rules for one account's Reels. Copy this file to
`~/.claude/instagram/reel-rules.md` and replace the rules with your own.
`/ig-reel` reads it before writing, and these rules override its defaults.

This copy is filled in for a faceless Islamic reminders account
(@thetazkiyyahguide) as an example.

## Always
- Pass the four checks in `/ig-reel` (outcome, curiosity loops, every line
  earns its place, change what the brain is processing every few seconds)
  before anything is voiced or rendered. No more than 4 seconds without a new
  visual beat.
- Faceless: silhouettes only, no faces.
- Background sound sits well under the voice. The voice is always clearly on top.
- End card mentions the free shapes book (no faces or animals, simple Arabic
  phrases for the family).
- Every Qur'an or hadith citation is checked against the source. A weak or
  broken chain is worded "It is reported that", with a source card before the
  end card.

## Never
- Never say "Follow The Tazkiyyah Guide" aloud. The follow line is on screen only.
